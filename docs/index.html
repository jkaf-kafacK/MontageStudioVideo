Features

Everything you need to publish, in one place

From a folder of raw clips to finished Shorts and YouTube videos. Your files never leave your computer.

Timeline editor

Set the start and end of each clip, crop, change speed, add transitions, titles, a logo and animated text.

Animated scenes

Title cards, key figures, lists, before and after, end screens. Use them as your intro or outro.

Top N rankings

Turn your best clips into a countdown Short with a numbered list and the current rank on screen.

Voice-over

Text to speech with Piper (free, offline), ElevenLabs, or your own voice cloned on your computer.

Automatic subtitles

From your script, an .srt file or Whisper speech recognition, with an optional karaoke effect.

Batch Shorts

Fill in a table or point to a folder, and get one finished video for every row or every file.

Quick tools

Create YouTube thumbnails, and turn any video into a vertical 9:16 Short in one click.

Fast export

Hardware encoding on NVIDIA, Apple and Intel graphics when available, CPU otherwise.

How it works

From raw clips to a finished video in three steps

The interface opens in your browser, but everything runs on your computer.

  1. Install

    Run the Windows installer. On first launch, Python and the components download once, automatically.

  2. Edit

    Import your clips and images, build the timeline, add scenes, voice-over and subtitles.

  3. Export

    Pick a format and export. Your videos are saved in Documents\Montage Studio Video.
